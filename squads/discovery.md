# Discovery squad

## Purpose

Reduce uncertainty about the user problem and desired outcome before committing to a solution.

## Compose from

- `product-discovery`
- `requirements-analysis` when decisions need to become testable scope
- A domain specialist when the product depends on regulated, operational, or industry-specific knowledge

## Handoff

Provide the problem statement, target users, evidence, current journey, desired outcome, constraints, assumptions, and questions that require a product decision. Do not present hypotheses as confirmed requirements.
