# Product discovery

Use when the problem, user, outcome, or evidence is unclear. Do not use this to bypass a product decision that belongs to the user or stakeholder.

1. Clarify the decision this discovery should support, target users, constraints, and time horizon.
2. Inspect available product, support, analytics, and research evidence. Separate observed facts, user reports, interpretations, and assumptions.
3. Describe the user's situation and desired progress. Read [Jobs to Be Done](references/jobs-to-be-done.md) when motivation or alternatives are unclear.
4. Map the current task sequence and friction when handoffs or failures matter. Read [journey mapping](references/journey-mapping.md) when useful.
5. State the strongest problem hypothesis, alternatives, risks, and the cheapest next evidence to collect.

## Deliverable

Return a short discovery brief with decision, user/problem, evidence with sources, assumptions, opportunities, unresolved questions, and a proposed next learning or delivery step. Do not claim user research or validation that did not happen.
